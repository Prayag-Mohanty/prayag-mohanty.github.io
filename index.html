Hey! I'm Prayag, an M.Tech student at IIT Bombay, advised by Prof. Virendra Singh at the Computer Architecture and Dependable Systems Lab (CADSL). I did my B.E. (Hons.) in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, Goa campus.

I'm interested in computer architecture, especially where GPUs waste on-chip resources. I'm currently working on address translation for multi-chip-module GPUs, and I previously designed a locality-aware victim cache for GPU L1 data caches. I also enjoy taking designs all the way from RTL to GDSII.

Outside academics, I play badminton, take part in quizzes, and write.
