About

I'm an M.Tech student in Electrical Engineering (Integrated Circuits & Systems) at IIT Bombay, advised by Prof. Virendra Singh at the Computer Architecture and Dependable Systems Lab. My research looks at where GPUs waste on-chip resources: virtual-memory translation across chiplets, L1 data-cache thrashing, and out-of-order scheduling.

Before this I did my B.E. (Hons.) in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, Goa campus. Outside the lab: quizzing, badminton and writing.

Research

Jul 2025 – Present

Address Translation for Multi-Chip-Module GPUs

M.Tech thesis. Investigating virtual-memory translation as a bottleneck when the TLB and page-walk hierarchy is split across dies. Proposes coupling TLB and cache replacement through dead-page prediction at the L1 TLB, prototyped on the MGvm MCM-GPU simulator.

Jan – May 2026

Locality-Aware Victim Cache for GPGPU L1 Data Caches

Co-designed a victim cache held in underused register-file capacity, paired with a warp scheduler that favours warps whose working set is still resident. Implemented in GPGPU-Sim on an NVIDIA Volta GV100 configuration for a 1.042× IPC gain over baseline. Presented at the Indo-Japanese Workshop on Trustable Cyber-Physical Cognitive Systems at IIT Bombay; submitted to ICCD 2026 (under review).

Jul – Dec 2025

Out-of-Order Scheduling for GPUs

M.Tech seminar. Surveyed seven out-of-order GPU proposals from ISCA, HPCA, TPDS and TACO (2016–2024) and compared their performance, power and area trade-offs. Showed that PTX-level simulation overstates available ILP, so SASS-level evaluation is the right baseline.

Publication: P. Mohanty and V. Singh, “Locality-Aware Victim Caching with Locality-Aware Warp Scheduling for GPUs,” submitted to IEEE ICCD 2026.

Hardware & architecture projects

2026

SS16: 16-bit superscalar processor

In-order superscalar core in SystemVerilog with a two-pass assembler, a golden instruction-set simulator and an automated co-simulation regression. Out-of-order extension (rename, ROB, issue queue) in progress.

2026

6-stage pipelined RISC processor and SAT-based ATPG

Full datapath with forwarding and interlocks in VHDL, plus SAT-based ATPG for the 74181 ALU that cut ATPG effort 3.67×.

2026

FHSS secure transmission with a hardware LWE engine

Learning-With-Errors encryption core on a PYNQ-Z2, interfaced over AXI-Lite and AXI-DMA for a 12.87× speedup over software, with frequency hopping through a custom GNU Radio module on a USRP.

2025

ML-driven LLC partitioning for CPU-GPU SoCs

Hardware perceptron that predicts per-core cache sensitivity to set LLC way-masks, gaining 8.36% on CPU at a 7.5% GPU cost over a static split. Instrumented gem5 for the motivating results.

2024

RTL-to-GDSII sequential MAC

16×16 shift-and-add multiplier taken through the full OpenLane flow on SkyWater sky130, closing at 5.53 ns setup with zero DRC violations and clean LVS.

2023

CNN inference accelerator on FPGA

B.E. thesis. AlexNet accelerator on a Zedboard (Zynq-7020) built with Vivado HLS: 0.74 GFLOPS at about 0.9 W, with roughly 10× lower power than comparable published designs.
